## Original Request

**Structured State Management Between Phases**
    *   **Current State**: Each development phase is triggered with the entire content of the proposal file. The AI has to re-parse the whole document to find the relevant information.
    *   **Opportunity**: Instead of passing the entire raw markdown file, you could update a structured **YAML frontmatter block** at the top of the proposal file. For example, after the `beta_council` runs, its JSON output could be injected directly into the frontmatter. The next role (`alpha_polish`) could then be prompted to specifically read from that section.
    *   **Benefit**: This creates a clean, machine-readable "state" that is passed between phases, making the process more robust, faster, and less prone to AI parsing errors.
